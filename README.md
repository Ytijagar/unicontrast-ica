# UniContrast — Information & Communication Architecture Project

Team ID 24

## Project Overview (PD1 Summary)
**UniContrast** is a web application that helps prospective and transferring students compare universities side by side and highlight meaningful trade-offs—covering tuition and currency-converted costs, campus country and region context, admission eligibility, personal priority weighting, application readiness checklists, pros/cons/dealbreakers, and a final shortlist decision summary.

* **Purpose:** Provide students with a structured comparison workspace where key differences and trade-offs between universities stand out clearly in one place, rather than being buried across scattered admissions websites, spreadsheets, and currency calculators.
* **Target Users:** High school seniors, transfer students, and international or exchange applicants evaluating and comparing university options.
* **Problem Solved:** University information is scattered across separate admissions pages, static rankings, and external converters, making it difficult to compare real-world trade-offs at a glance.
